01 — The approach

Good copy starts before the first sentence.

The goal isn’t to make words sound clever. It’s to make the value clear, address the buyer’s real questions, and guide the next action.

Understand the buyer

Clarify who the offer is for, what they want, what holds them back, and what they need to believe before acting.

Build the argument

Shape the message around the problem, the offer’s value, credible proof, objections, and one clear call to action.

03 — Selected work

See the thinking behind the copy.

Illustrative practice projects showing how I approach messaging, clarity, and persuasion.

Website rewriteHealth & wellness concept

ZenFlow — From feature list to customer problem

Original direction
“ZenFlow is a yoga app with lots of classes for all levels. We have breathing exercises and meditation content too.”
Revised direction
“Your nervous system is running on empty. ZenFlow brings yoga, breathwork, and meditation into short sessions made for people who feel they don’t have time to slow down.”
Strategic decision

Lead with the audience’s lived problem, explain the offer in plain language, and make the intended user feel understood before listing features.

Ad conceptSupplement brand concept

SolBody — Lead with a recognisable moment

Generic starting point
“Buy SolBody Magnesium Glycinate. High quality. Fast shipping. Order now.”
Revised direction
“Looking for a magnesium supplement? Start with the label. SolBody puts ingredient transparency and third-party testing front and centre.”
Strategic decision

Use a relevant buying concern as the hook, then support the message with verifiable product details instead of promising a health outcome.

Email copyHabit app concept

VitaTrack — Welcome email opening

Opening sample
“You’ve taken the first step. Now let’s make tracking feel useful — not like another task on your to-do list. Start with one habit you want to understand better.”
Strategic decision

Reduce pressure for a new user, give them one manageable next step, and frame the product around usefulness rather than guilt.

NourishWell — Search-led article

Article concept
“10 Foods That May Help Support a Balanced, Anti-Inflammatory Eating Pattern”
Strategic decision

Match search intent with a clear, practical structure while keeping health-related language measured and avoiding unsupported guarantees.
